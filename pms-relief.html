PMS relief

Small comforts for difficult days

None of these will make PMS disappear, and that is okay. They are simple, low-effort things that many people find take the edge off. Try one at a time and notice what actually helps your body.

🌼

Gentle reminder. Home remedies can ease discomfort but they do not cure PMS. If your symptoms are severe, last longer than your period, or disrupt work, school or sleep, please speak with a qualified healthcare professional. You deserve proper support, not just coping.

When it is worth talking to someone

Self-care has limits, and reaching out is a practical step — not an overreaction.

  • Pain that is not eased by warmth, rest or over-the-counter pain relief.
  • Symptoms that stop you going to work, school or seeing people.
  • Very heavy bleeding, or periods that last much longer than usual.
  • Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.
  • Mood changes that feel overwhelming or last beyond your period.
  • Symptoms that are getting worse cycle after cycle.
