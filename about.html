About Bloom Cycle

A calmer way to understand your cycle

Bloom Cycle began with a simple idea: cycle tracking should feel like a kind conversation, not a spreadsheet of things you failed to do.

Why we keep it simple

Most cycle apps ask for an account, a subscription and a lot of personal detail. Bloom Cycle does the opposite. You can log today, glance at your phase, read a practical tip and close the tab. That is enough.

Everything you enter stays in your own browser. There is no server, no sign-up and no tracking. Clear your data whenever you like and it is genuinely gone.

How the numbers work

A quick look under the hood, in plain language.

1️⃣

Cycle length

Days between the first day of one period and the first day of the next. The average uses every completed cycle you have logged.

2️⃣

Phases

The menstrual phase covers your period days. Ovulation is estimated about 14 days before the next period, and the follicular and luteal phases fill the rest.

3️⃣

Insights

Symptoms and moods are counted per cycle, so a comment like “cramps in 3 of your last 4 cycles” comes from your actual entries.

Questions you might have

Short answers, no fine print.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's localStorage under the key bloomcycle.v1. It stays after a refresh and is removed if you clear site data or press “Clear all data” in the tracker.

Can I use one account on two devices?

Not currently. Because nothing is uploaded, each browser keeps its own copy. This is the trade-off for having no account and no server.

How accurate is the next period prediction?

It is as accurate as your cycle is regular. It is an estimate calculated from your average cycle length — never a guarantee, and never a form of contraception.

Is this suitable if my cycles are irregular?

You can still track symptoms and moods, and the analysis will show the spread of your cycle lengths. If irregularity is new or worrying, please talk to a healthcare professional.

Does Bloom Cycle diagnose PMS or any condition?

No. It offers general education about self-care and helps you notice patterns. It cannot diagnose anything and does not replace professional care.

Can I edit something I logged by mistake?

Yes. Open the tracker, pick that date from the list or the calendar, change the details and save — or delete the entry entirely.

Ready when you are

You do not need a full history to begin. The first day of your last period is enough for a starting estimate.
